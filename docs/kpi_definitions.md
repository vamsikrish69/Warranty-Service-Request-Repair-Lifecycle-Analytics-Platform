# KPI Definitions

## Service Request KPIs

### Total Service Requests

Number of service requests created.

### Service Request Conversion Rate

```text
Service Requests ÷ Customer Contacts
```

Measures the percentage of customer contacts that resulted in a service request.

### Request Age

```text
Current Date - Contact Date
```

Measures how long requests remain active.

---

## Repair KPIs

### Repair Completion Rate

```text
Completed Repairs ÷ Total Repairs
```

Measures repair execution effectiveness.

### Average Repair Turnaround Time

```text
Repair Completion Time - Repair Start Time
```

Measures repair efficiency.

### Part Delay Rate

```text
Repairs With Part Delay ÷ Total Repairs
```

Measures supply chain impact on repair performance.

---

## SLA KPIs

### SLA Adherence Rate

```text
Requests Meeting SLA ÷ Total Requests
```

Measures SLA compliance.

### SLA Breach Rate

```text
Requests Breaching SLA ÷ Total Requests
```

Measures service delivery failures.

---

## Warranty KPIs

### Warranty Utilization Rate

```text
Service Requests ÷ Eligible Warranty Assets
```

Measures warranty usage.

### Active Warranty Service Requests

Service requests created while warranty coverage was active.

### Expired Warranty Contacts

Customer contacts received after warranty expiration.

---

## Customer Support KPIs

### First Contact Resolution Rate

```text
Contacts Resolved During Initial Interaction ÷ Total Contacts
```

Measures support effectiveness.

### Escalation Rate

```text
Escalated Requests ÷ Total Requests
```

Measures operational complexity.

### Repeat Service Request Rate

```text
Repeat Requests ÷ Total Requests
```

Measures recurring issues and resolution quality.

---

## Product Quality KPIs

### Top Issue Categories

Most frequently reported issue types.

Examples:

```text
NO_POWER
NO_POST
NO_VIDEO
NO_BOOT
OSRI
WINDOWS_ISSUE
```

### Issue-to-Service Conversion Rate

```text
Service Requests ÷ Issue Contacts
```

Measures which issues are most likely to require repair intervention.
